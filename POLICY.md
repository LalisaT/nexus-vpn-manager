# Acceptable Use & Takedown Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

## 1. Purpose and Intended Use
NexusVPN is an open-source networking and diagnostic utility developed strictly for:
- Securing personal network communications on public or untrusted Wi-Fi.
- Researching encryption, transport protocols, and network performance.
- Legitimate privacy protection in compliance with applicable laws.

## 2. Prohibited Uses
Users and contributors are strictly prohibited from using this software to:
- Bypass mobile carrier billing, data tariff accounting, or zero-rating restrictions (toll fraud / theft of telecommunications services).
- Gain unauthorized access to networks, servers, or computing systems.
- Conduct distributed denial-of-service (DDoS) attacks, port scanning, or vulnerability exploitation.
- Distribute malware, spyware, or facilitate any illegal activity.
- Violate the Acceptable Use Policies (AUP) or Terms of Service of their internet service provider (ISP) or telecommunications operator.

## 3. Compliance and Local Law
Users are solely responsible for ensuring that their deployment and use of this software complies with all local, national, and international laws, as well as contractual agreements with telecommunications providers. The developers assume no liability for misuse.

## 4. Takedown and Abuse Reporting
If you are a network operator, rights holder, or legal authority with concerns regarding this software, its deployment, or its distribution:
- **Contact:** Open an issue on GitHub at [https://github.com/LalisaT/nexus-vpn-manager/issues](https://github.com/LalisaT/nexus-vpn-manager/issues)
- **Procedure:** Provide detailed information regarding the specific concern, legal jurisdiction, or alleged violation.
- **Commitment:** Legitimate notices of abuse, intellectual property concerns, or legal non-compliance will be reviewed promptly, and appropriate corrective actions (including repository updates, feature deprecation, or cessation of public distribution) will be taken.
